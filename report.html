Voice report

Sing one steady “ahh” at a comfortable pitch and medium-loud volume for ~6 seconds. The analysis measures pitch steadiness, breathiness, vibrato, and resonance, then tells you what to work on. Hold the phone about 30 cm (a forearm) from your mouth, in a quiet room.
